QStory shows historical places around you and turns a walk into a hunt: pick a place on the map, follow the compass and claim the find when you get there.

OPEN SOURCES
Places come from databases kept by historians, local researchers and volunteers:
• PastVu — old photographs tied to a place and a year;
• Wikipedia — articles about places nearby;
• Wikidata — cultural heritage sites;
• OpenStreetMap — monuments, memorials, ruins, forts.
Every source can be turned off. The app has no server of its own.

PLACE HUNTS
• Compass, distance and an ETA based on your real walking speed.
• Steps and distance walked for every find.
• A place is counted automatically when you step into its radius.
• A "Nearby" list and a "Walk there" button for your maps app.

STATISTICS
• Steps for the day, the week and all time, a daily goal and streaks.
• This week vs last week, charts by day, hour and weekday.
• Hunt records, explored area, 16 achievements.
• Export all your data as JSON.

QR CODES
Scan QR codes on plaques and share places with friends.

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no analytics. Routes, steps and statistics stay on the device. Only the map area coordinates are sent to the open sources to find places nearby.

Background tracking and daily reminders are off by default and only you can turn them on.
